Dave's Lawn Desk: privacy

Dave's Lawn Desk is a demo of a text-message booking assistant for a fictional lawn-mowing business. Everyone and every booking in it is made up.

Google Calendar

If you choose Connect Google Calendar, the demo asks Google for one permission: to create its own calendar and manage the events in that calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created). It then creates a calendar named "Dave's Lawn Desk" in your account and copies the demo's two weeks of fictional bookings into it.

  • It cannot read, change, or delete your other calendars or events.
  • It stores only what it needs to keep that calendar in step: an encrypted Google refresh token, the calendar's id, and the ids of the events it added.
  • It never shares this with anyone and uses it for nothing else.
  • Disconnect deletes the "Dave's Lawn Desk" calendar with every event it added, revokes the permission, and deletes the stored token.

You can also remove the permission any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Everything else

Sign-in uses a shared access code and a nickname. The demo keeps the texts you type and the fictional bookings for your nickname so you can come back to them; Reset all clears them.
